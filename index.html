The model lattice, all at once

Every model this document could build from five on/off ingredients — drift, noise, leverage, living expenses, constant‑debt‑to‑assets maintenance — laid out as one graph. This is the alternative being weighed against a toggle‑based node page: can seeing the whole shape at once, rather than switching flags one page at a time, communicate the space faster? No node has real content here on purpose — this page tests only whether the graph itself reads.

Layout. Columns are ingredient count, left (0, the constant‑price model) to right (5, every ingredient at once). Edges join states differing by exactly one ingredient — a Hasse diagram of the partial lattice described in knowledge_map_plan.md. Within each column, node order is a barycenter heuristic run over a few sweeps to reduce edge crossings; the two 7‑node middle columns still cross a great deal, and that clutter is left visible rather than hidden — it is the honest answer to what this prototype is for.

Treated vs. untreated, provisional. The document's own sections were read to make this call, not guessed: the no‑leverage price models (pure exponential, random walk, geometric Brownian motion, §I–III) are treated, as is the deterministic leveraged branch (§II.3–II.4, II.8: leveraged growth, constant debt‑to‑assets leverage, expense‑funded debt) and the stochastic leveraged branch (§IV, IV.2, Va: leverage under uncertainty and its living‑expenses extension). Pure‑noise‑with‑leverage combinations, and the remaining combinations lacking both drift and noise, are marked untreated as exotica or as degenerate (leverage on a non‑moving price). One node lacking both is marked treated all the same: the living‑expenses‑only node, a pile of money earning nothing and spent down, which the toggle page derives in full on both clocks — no section of the linear document carries it, and the node's own text says so. One seam is genuinely blurred: Part IV.2's “maintained assumption” (leverage held constant) is definitionally the same condition as this graph's constant‑debt‑to‑assets flag, so both the flagged and unflagged stochastic‑leverage nodes are marked treated even though only the flagged one has a name of its own in the text. This boundary is an editorial call, not something the document settles.
